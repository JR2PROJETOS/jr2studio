About me

Jair Vieira
Architect & Urbanist | Revit & BIM | Brazil, remote worldwide

I am an architect with 20+ years of experience in residential, commercial and mixed-use projects. I have run my own architecture practice since 2002, and I work every day in Revit, AutoCAD and SketchUp.

Before JR2 Studio, I was Director of Urban Planning for the city of Dourados, Brazil, where I reviewed architectural and technical documentation, zoning and building regulations. I also taught architecture and urbanism at the university level.

I studied Architecture and Urban Planning (UNIGRAN) and hold an MBA in Structural Engineering and Foundations (IPOG). That mix helps me read a project as an architect and as a builder.

I work remotely with architecture studios, in writing and with clear progress updates, and I can adjust my hours to U.S. time zones.
