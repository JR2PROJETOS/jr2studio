The person behind JR2 Studio

Architectural experience meets hands-on BIM production.

Jair Vieira
Architect & Urbanist | Revit & BIM | Brazil, remote worldwide

I am a Brazilian architect with 20+ years of experience in residential, commercial and mixed-use projects. Since 2002 I have run my own architecture practice, combining design, technical documentation and multidisciplinary coordination.

I was Director of Urban Planning for the city of Dourados, where I reviewed architectural and technical documentation, zoning and building regulations. I also taught architecture and urbanism at the university level.

I studied Architecture and Urban Planning (UNIGRAN) and hold an MBA in Structural Engineering and Foundations (IPOG). That mix helps me read a project as an architect and as a builder.

I work with Revit, AutoCAD and Navisworks, in writing and with clear progress updates, and I can adjust my hours to U.S. time zones.
